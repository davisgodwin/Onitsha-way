# v0.46.0 — Real buildings for the landmarks; enter first, then work; hotel rooms; stage 7 begins

## The landmarks get real buildings
Until now the two cathedrals, the palace, CKC and Shanahan University were only a name on the map (Holy Trinity was
a generic church with no way in). Each now has its own building on its own grounds, facing a real road, with a door
you walk up to ("… — go in"):
- **Cathedral Basilica of the Most Holy Trinity**: built on the spot of its old footprint, on raised ground with
  front steps: twin west towers with spires and gold crosses, a long cream nave and aisles with buttresses and
  coloured lancet windows, a rose window over the great door, an apse. Inside: columns down the nave, a painted apse,
  pointed-arch windows, and a shrine with the relics of Blessed Iwene Tansi.
- **All Saints' Cathedral**: a large modernist block with a portico of tall concrete arches, coloured glass, and a tall
  rectangular bell tower with an open belfry and a cross. Inside: coloured glass between concrete arches, the
  bishop's chair.
- **Ime Obi**: a walled red-and-white compound: gatehouse, courtyard with palms, and the palace hall with a deep
  veranda of white columns under a red roof. Inside is the visitors' hall from v0.45. Stylised; the Obi is never shown.
- **Christ the King College**: a walled school (founded 1933): gatehouse, administration block, two-storey classroom
  blocks with verandas round a quadrangle, a flagpole and a football pitch.
- **Shanahan University** (the map had it as "Shanaham"): a gated campus: entrance arch, a four-storey senate building
  with a columned portico and a glass front, two faculty blocks round a lawn with a fountain, a chapel, palms. Inside:
  admissions, a tiered lecture theatre, the library and the staff room.

What I could check about the real buildings: Holy Trinity was built 1930–35 on raised ground; All Saints (begun 1949,
dedicated 1992) has a rectangular bell tower, many concrete arches and coloured glass. The rest is stylised: these are
game buildings, not surveyed replicas. Their grounds were reserved before the city's houses are placed, and **no
existing building was removed** except Holy Trinity's own generic footprint, so every home, flat and shop you rent
stays where it was. Where there wasn't room at the mapped point, the building stands on the nearest clear plot and its
map pin moved with it (CKC is about 330 m from its old pin, the palace about 130 m).

## Go in first, then work
- **Metadav ICT Hub** has a new building: two storeys, glass ground floor with green fins, a cantilevered upper floor
  with a lit strip, solar panels, planters and a bike rack.
- **Every workplace** now works the same way: the door says "go in"; applying, working shifts, training and buying
  equipment happen at the desks inside. The market buyers' desk, the property office, the motors and mechanic workshop
  (two cars on the floor), the dispatch office (parcel shelves), the catering training kitchen and the fashion
  workshop (sewing machines) each have their own room.
- Hotels and the palace, churches and schools: you walk in through their doors too.

## Hotel rooms
Each hotel has a corridor of **four rooms you can walk into**: **Standard**, **Deluxe**, **Executive** and a **Suite**,
furnished to their price, with the price over each door (it depends on the hotel). Book at the door or at
reception: **a 3-hour rest** (40% of the night) or **a night** (you sleep until 7:00). You lie down on that room's
bed.

## Smaller fixes
- **Car doors**: only the door on your side opens when you get in, and only the one you get out of.
- The eatery by the Main Market is now **Mama Blessing's Kitchen**.

## Stage 7 (first part): the professions, university, freelance work
- **Shanahan University**: enrol at admissions (game fees) in Medicine and Surgery, Nursing Science, Law, Education,
  Computer Science or Mass Communication; attend one lecture a day (weekdays 8:00–17:00) in the lecture theatre;
  study in the library; **graduate** with a degree.
- **The professions** need their degree, and you apply inside their building:
  - **Doctor** and **Nurse**: the hospitals' staff door. A shift is a patient case: a child with fever, a keke
    accident, chest pain (doctor); the ward round, triage at reception (nurse).
  - **Teacher**: the CKC staff room. A shift is a class (SS2 mathematics, marking scripts).
  - **Lecturer**: the Shanahan staff room (any degree, coding, a laptop).
  - **Lawyer**: the robing room at the Magistrate Court. A shift is a brief (a trader accused of theft, a tenancy).
  Each has five ranks, choices graded right or wrong (you're paid more for getting them right), and every third
  shift an honest-or-corrupt choice (bribes for the queue, selling drugs, changing marks, coaching a witness) with
  warnings and dismissal.
- **Freelance**:
  - **Journalist** for The Onitsha Courier: a new phone app. Take an assignment (traffic at Upper Iweka, prices at
    Ochanja, election results, a new public project), go there, interview people and take photos, file the story;
    it's paid and appears in the paper. A Mass Communication degree pays more.
  - **DJ** and **hypeman**: the DJ booth at the nightclubs: play a set or grab the mic, once a night each.
- All of it is for adults.
