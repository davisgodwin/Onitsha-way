# v0.53.0: more GRA homes, busy filling stations, gates that open, free furniture placement

## More homes in GRA
140 more walled houses in GRA Phase I and II are now to rent or buy. With the earlier ones, the test counted 44
listings in Phase I and 58 in Phase II. They're listed after all the existing homes, so every home you already rent
or own keeps its number and place.

## Filling stations come alive
At the filling stations near you (the nearest three, within about 170 m):
- **The cars**: they turn in from the road, drive round to a pump and stop.
- **The pump attendants**: an attendant walks over, holds the nozzle (with the hose running from the pump) and fills
  up. Then the car drives out, and the next one comes in.
- **The staff**: two attendants at the pump islands and one at the mini-mart door.
- **Busier by day**: fewer cars late at night.
- **For show**: the cars don't collide, so you can walk through them.

## Compound gates open
Every compound gate (about 22,500 of them) is now a real sliding gate. It slides open as you walk or drive up to it
(a little sooner in a car) and closes behind you. Before, the gate was painted shut and you passed straight through
it. Only the gates near you are drawn as moving gates.

## Buy mode: place things where you like
Furniture can now overlap other furniture, fittings and the room's walls. The only rules left are:
- the item stays inside your home;
- it doesn't block a door or the stairs.

Overlapping things stay where you put them when you leave and come back. Before, they could be moved to storage.

## No kiosks in front of entrances
Kiosks and their generators that stood in front of a compound gate, a house or shop door, or a venue's entrance are
gone: 651 of 6,445 kiosks. That includes a filling station's whole forecourt. The test found no kiosk left in front
of any gate.
