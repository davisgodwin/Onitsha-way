            About Onitsha Way

            An open-world city-life simulator inspired by Onitsha, Anambra State. Street geometry and building footprints come from OpenStreetMap. Prices, services, people and interiors are designed for play; some entrances are approximate.

            • Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).
            • 3D engine three.js (MIT).
            • Signboard lettering Anton by Vernon Adams, bundled via Fontsource (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
            • Characters, textures, vehicles and sound generated procedurally in code for this project — no third-party art or audio assets. Vehicle designs are generic.
            • Onitsha expressions supplied by the developer; meanings are unverified until checked with local speakers. Example dialogue lines are original to the game.
