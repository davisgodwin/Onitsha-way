# Milestone 16 · v0.16.0 — A local city with real players

Local account registration, shared avatars and player-driven vehicles, nearby/team/direct text, WebRTC push-to-talk voice, mutual friendship/relationships/dates, private home invitations, car passenger invitations, abstract legal/risky co-op tasks, and seven-day test business billboards.

One ₦3,000 slot lasts seven days. Multiple slots extend time consecutively at one chosen area. All payments and ad approval are explicitly local TEST simulations; there are no real charges or live payment-provider integrations.

Environment improvements: procedural cloud layers, sun/night detail, wind-animated roadside grass, and refined laterite mud/ruts. The mall forecourt and vehicle access lanes are kept clear of streamed grass and the community billboard.

Run `npm ci` then `npm run local`, and open http://localhost:5173. Use two different browser profiles/accounts for same-machine multiplayer and voice testing. Preserve/export old saves; account progress is isolated from the old offline save.

Read LOCAL-MULTIPLAYER-16.md, START-HERE.txt and QA_LOCAL_16.md for operating instructions, validation and production boundaries. This is not a public MMO or production real-money ad platform.
