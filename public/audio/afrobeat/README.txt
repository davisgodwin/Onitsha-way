Created procedurally for this game release: new melodies, chords, bass lines and
synthesized percussion. No sampled artist recordings, vocals, covers or external
streaming services. Described as Afrobeat-style game instrumentals, not tracks
by any commercial recording artist. No third-party music attribution required.
Generation source: scripts/compose-venue-music.py (Python + NumPy + FFmpeg).

Use: play in open bars within 30 game metres and in the nightclub interior.
Controls: venue menu / DJ -> Music: mute, next track, and music volume.
The game's master volume and menu attenuation also apply. Audio starts after a
click or keypress, as required by browsers. Track list cycles automatically.
Files are local to the game and precached by the production service worker.
