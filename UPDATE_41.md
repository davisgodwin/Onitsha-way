# v0.41.0 — Open life, stage 2: banks and loans, your own businesses, transport companies, buses

Stage 2 of the open-life plan. Built and tested single-player, as agreed. All money is game money; the banks and
every business name are fictional.

## Banks
- **Two banks in town**, each with a branch on the map: **Nkwo Trust Bank** (near Main Market) and **Bridgehead
  Savings & Loans** (near Upper Iweka). Find them with the map search "Banks" or the phone's Bank app.
- **Open an account** at a branch (₦500). Your account is separate from the cash you carry: deposit, withdraw, and
  earn a little interest each day (0.1% a day, only on up to ₦2,000,000).
- **Credit score** (300–850, you start at 520). It rises with a steady income, a clear police record, time with the
  bank and paying on time. It falls when you miss payments.
- **Loans**:
  - *Personal loan*: up to half of what you earned in the last 14 days (max ₦150,000), 10% interest, repaid over 14
    days. Needs a score of 450 and a clean police record.
  - *Secured loan*: borrow up to 60% of the value of your vehicle or your home, 6% interest, over 28 days. Score 400.
  - A daily instalment is taken from your account. If the account can't cover it, the payment is missed: a 5% late
    fee and a lower score. **Three missed payments and the loan is in default**: the bank takes what it can from
    your account and then your cash, a secured loan's vehicle or home is **repossessed**, and you can't borrow again
    until it's settled. You can repay early from the Loans screen.
- **A pledged vehicle can't be sold** until its loan is repaid.
- Phone > **Bank**: balance, credit score, loans and what's owed (repay from here), send money to your businesses,
  find a branch, recent transactions.

## Your own shop
- **8 shops to let** around town (map search "Shops to let"). Walk up and choose what to open: a provisions shop,
  a buka (food), a beer parlour, a phone & accessories shop or a fashion boutique.
- **Rent** (registration ₦5,000 + the first week's rent) or **buy** the premises. You pick a name and it goes up on
  a sign over the shop. Up to 3 businesses.
- **The business has its own account.** Put money in from your cash or bank, take profit out.
- **Buy stock** from the wholesaler (paid from the business account), **set prices** (cheap brings more customers,
  premium earns more per sale), **set opening hours**.
- **Hire attendants** (up to 4). Each candidate has a name, a skill and references. Hiring sets aside 3 days of wages
  in **escrow** (a held contract): wages are paid from it every day and topped up from the business account. Can't
  pay and they quit. Let them go any time and the rest of the escrow comes back.
- **Customers come every hour** the shop is open and someone is serving (you, standing in the shop, or an attendant).
  How many depends on the area, the hour, your prices, how happy past customers were, and the shop's reputation.
  Run out of stock and customers are turned away unhappy. An attendant with poor references may pocket some takings
  when you're not there.
- **Rent, wages and spoilage are due whether you sell or not**: buka food spoils overnight; miss rent twice and
  you're evicted. **You can lose money.**
- **Time away counts**: when you come back, the hours you were gone are worked out (up to 2 days).
- **Reports**: each day's sales, customers served and turned away, wages, rent and notes.

## Transport companies and buses
- Phone > My business > **Start a transport company**. Buy a keke, minibus or bus at a car stand, **put it to work**,
  and **hire a driver**. Each day the driver brings back the takings ("delivery") after fuel; the vehicle wears and
  breaks down at 80% damage until a mechanic fixes it. A careless driver brings back less. Keke ~₦9,000 a day,
  minibus ~₦22,000, coaster ~₦28,000, bus ~₦40,000 before fuel and wages.
- **Drive a bus yourself**: minibuses and buses now carry fare-paying passengers like the keke, with more seats
  and bus fares over longer distances. Phone > My business > **Bus route**: pick one of the city's lines and you
  only stop for passengers at its stops. **Hire a conductor** for the day (₦1,500): they call passengers from
  further away and collect fares as people board.
- **Selling a vehicle** removes it from your company.

## Profile and reputation
- Running a shop makes you a **Business owner** and a company a **Transport operator**, with their own ranks
  (New in business → Established → Well known → Big business). Bus driving counts as its own work.
- Your **wealth** now counts your bank balance (minus what you owe), your business accounts and premises you own.

## Saves
- Save version **19**. Old saves get an empty bank (no account), no businesses and no contracts. Cash, vehicles,
  homes, jobs and everything else are kept.

## Not in this stage
- Police, EFCC, crime against NPCs, Hustle Kingdoms and the magistrate court are stage 3.
- Money is still simulated on your device (server checks are stage 4). Other players can't use your shop yet.
- Shops don't have customers walking in as 3D people: trade is worked out each hour and shown in reports.
