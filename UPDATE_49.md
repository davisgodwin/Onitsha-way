# v0.49.0: a new map screen, the city filled in rows of walled houses, more places, traffic lights and wardens

## The map
The full map is now a light street map, laid out like a phone maps app.
- **Search** for any place, street, area, bus stop or COMING SOON town. Pick a result and the map goes there and
  marks it for the GPS.
- **Chips** show one kind of place at a time:
  - Homes;
  - Food & markets;
  - Bars, clubs & hotels;
  - Shopping & cars;
  - Services;
  - Police & government;
  - Transport (the bus lines stand out);
  - Fun & sport;
  - Jobs.
- **The key** (the "i" button) explains every colour and line, with how many places of each kind there are.
- **Every place has a name on the map.** The neighbourhoods have names too: GRA Phase I, GRA Phase II, Fegge,
  Bridgehead, Awada, Trans Nkissi Layout, and Onitsha North and Onitsha South when zoomed out. GRA is shaded green.
- **Zoom in** and the streets, every building and the compound walls are drawn sharp. The houses on empty land
  now show on the map too.
- **COMING SOON land** is shaded grey. Asaba, Nkpor, Obosi and Okpoko are named in red.
- **Other details:**
  - a blue "you" dot with the way you're facing;
  - a red pin for your destination;
  - a north arrow and a scale bar;
  - the area at the middle of the map, shown at the bottom.
- **On phones**, the same map fits the screen, with the buttons within thumb reach.
- **The small map in the corner** uses the same colours.

## The empty land is filled, in rows and columns
- **New houses:** about 7,000 on the low setting, 12,000 on medium and 15,000 on high. They are laid along every
  street:
  - plots of one standard width side by side, one standard depth behind each other, up to five rows deep;
  - squared to the street;
  - each house the street's standard size, the same distance back from the street.
- **The kinds of street:**
  - GRA: big plots, villas and two-storey houses, tall finished walls, black gates;
  - the old districts: narrow plots, more two-storey houses;
  - everywhere else: ordinary family plots.
- **Every one has a walled compound with a gate on the street side.**
- **The houses already standing on empty land** now have the standard size for their street and stand at the same
  distance from it. That lines them up.
- **Walls and gates everywhere:**
  - every house that had no wall now has one;
  - about 3,000 more houses and small businesses from the map got a wall and a gate, which stands in front of the
    door;
  - a wall is only added where it doesn't cross a road, a tree or a prop, or shut in a door, a counter, a person or
    a parked car.
- **More homes to rent or buy:** 160 of the walled houses are on the market. You can rent or buy them like the
  others; GRA homes cost a bit more.
- **The house under the Niger Bridge ramp** (and two more at the bridge) is gone. No new house is built under a
  bridge deck or flyover.

## COMING SOON you can see
- **Every road that leaves Onitsha (51 of them)** now ends in a big gantry with a red-and-white roadblock under it.
  The gantries are 9–16 m wide and up to 8 m high, and say "TOWARDS NKPOR / OBOSI / OKPOKO · COMING SOON".
- **Between the roads:** 18 tall billboards along the line say "ONITSHA ENDS HERE".
- The boards glow a little at night.
- Each gantry is on the map.

## Traffic
- **Traffic lights at 208 busy junctions.** Each junction lets one direction go at a time: 13 s green, 3 s amber
  and a short all-red. Drivers stop at the line on red, and on amber if they can stop in time. The lights hang from
  a mast arm over the lane, and only the ones near you are drawn.
- **Traffic wardens at the 12 busiest junctions:** by the markets, Upper Iweka, Bridgehead and the mall. Each stands
  on a stand under a red umbrella and sends each direction through in turn.
- **Vehicles no longer drive through each other:**
  - they keep a gap they can stop in;
  - they see a car that has just turned in front of them;
  - they don't enter a junction while someone is crossing it;
  - if two vehicles ever touch, the one behind is held back at once.
- **Tested:** in two simulated minutes at Upper Iweka, no vehicle drove into another. Before this change there
  were dozens of bumps.

## Saves
- **The save version is unchanged (23).** Every home, resident, shop and business from v0.48 keeps its number and
  its spot. A browser test compares the old map and the new one.
- **The exceptions:**
  - In v0.48 the new part of the map (GRA and the north and east) had houses on empty land. Those are replaced by
    the new rows, so the neighbours who stood at those gates are gone. A friendship with one of them is kept in your
    save, but that person no longer appears.
  - Three houses at the Niger Bridge ramp are gone; a neighbour who lived in one of them no longer has a home.
